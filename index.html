More Than a Gift Shop

We are a shared marketplace where independent makers showcase their passion.

Berlin Kiosks is Connecticut's premier destination for handcrafted quality. Instead of generic retail, we provide a warm, inviting space for local creators to rent a kiosk and connect directly with the community. Every purchase supports a local dream.
